# Code of Conduct

Mōchirīī contributors are expected to keep collaboration respectful, safe, and
focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate constructively and assume good intent while evaluating evidence.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, personal boundaries, and cultural
  differences.
- Give actionable technical feedback without harassment or personal attacks.
- Credit contributions accurately and disclose conflicts of interest.
- Protect member data and report security concerns through the private channel
  described in `SECURITY.md`.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, credential or
private-data disclosure, impersonation, retaliation, and deliberate disruption
are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers. Maintainers will
limit access to the report, assess it consistently, preserve relevant evidence,
and take proportionate action. Actions may include a warning, removal of content,
temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project spaces.
